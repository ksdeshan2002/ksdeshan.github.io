Introduction

Hi, this is the modern era, and the world is increasingly powered by AI. In fact, even this website was built with the help of several AI prompts.

I aspire to pursue the field of Embedded Systems, combining it with Edge AI to build intelligent systems that can think and make decisions closer to where data is generated.

One thing that makes me different is the way I learn. I enjoy understanding technical concepts as stories rather than just facts, and I love sharing those stories with others. That's why you'll find technical storytelling throughout this website, and I plan to continue creating more in the future.

Another thing that drives me is curiosity. I rarely stop at simply finding a solution—I enjoy digging deeper until I understand the root cause of the problem. For me, solving a problem is only part of the journey; understanding why it happens is what truly matters.

Currently, I'm pursuing a degree in Computer Systems Engineering at SLIIT, Sri Lanka, with the goal of combining AI and Embedded Systems to build the next generation of intelligent edge devices.
